## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **UI/UX and Product Designer** from Ghaziabad, India, with **3+ years of experience** building research-driven digital products for **eCommerce, marketplaces, SaaS, CRM, and ERP** platforms. I currently work at **Webkul Software**, where I design web and mobile interfaces end-to-end — from user research and journey mapping to design systems and developer handoffs.

Lately I've been deep into **agentic AI workflows** — Figma MCP, AI-assisted prototyping, and vibe coding — to turn product ideas into testable interfaces faster.
